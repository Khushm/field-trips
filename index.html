🛢️ Field Trips & Industry Visits

Hands-on learning through visits to energy facilities, drilling sites, and sustainability projects

Connecting Classroom to Industry

Our Petroleum Engineering Minor and Center for Subsurface Energy & Sustainability organize regular field trips to give students firsthand experience with industry operations. From drilling sites in the Utica and Marcellus Shale regions to cutting-edge research laboratories, these experiences bridge the gap between theory and practice.
